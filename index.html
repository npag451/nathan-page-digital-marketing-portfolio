Conversion Optimisation

Recliner Finder Tool — Doubling Conversion Through Better UX

La-Z-Boy's recliner finder quiz was sitting at 8–9% sign-up rate against a 10% target. I diagnosed two root causes: quiz drop-off after four questions, and a sign-up CTA with no genuine value exchange. By shortening the quiz and working with Preezie and Mailchimp to deliver quiz results by email — a real reason to hand over your address — conversion rate jumped to 15% in a single month.
