About

The person behind the numbers.

What I like about performance marketing is the loop. You ship something, you watch the numbers, you work out why they moved, and you act on it. Real campaigns, real budgets, results you can see the moment they shift.

Data tells you what happened. Insight tells you why. The job is knowing what to do next.

For three years I built and ran the full digital campaign infrastructure for La-Z-Boy NZ/AU and Hunter Home NZ at the same time, across two markets, from brief through to post-campaign analysis. I don't wait to be told what to do next. I find it.

Originally from New Zealand, now based in London. I ask the awkward question early rather than explain the miss later, and when something's working I don't sit on it.

Selected Work

Four campaigns, four problems solved.

A note on the numbers: all figures are in NZD. For furniture retail I report to CPA, not ROAS, because the online conversion is a store-locator click and the sale completes in the showroom. Optimising to the click that actually precedes a store visit is more honest than chasing a revenue figure the platform can't really see.
Conversion · Mailchimp · Preezie

A recliner finder quiz that earned its place.

The quiz was converting at around 9%. I cut it back to the questions that actually predicted a recommendation, then used Preezie and Mailchimp to email each person their result. That turned it from a gimmick into a genuine value exchange: answer a few questions, get a real recommendation in your inbox.

9% → 15%

Acquisition · Google Ads · Demand Gen

Out of traffic at year-end, so I changed the engine.

With end-of-financial-year targets to hit and Performance Max traffic plateauing, I moved budget into Google Demand Gen to open up reach the existing setup couldn't find. It roughly doubled traffic, and because raw volume from a broader campaign type always risks quality, I didn't just let it run: I tracked the incoming quality and pulled back on the levers bringing in the wrong visitors, so the extra traffic stayed useful rather than just bigger.
