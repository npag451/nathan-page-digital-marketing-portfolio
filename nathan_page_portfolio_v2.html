Conversion Optimisation — La-Z-Boy NZ

Recliner Finder Tool — Doubling Conversion by Fixing the Wrong Thing

The quiz had a traffic problem on paper — sign-up rate sitting at 8–9% against a 10% target. But the traffic was fine. I dug into completion rates at each quiz stage and found two separate issues: drop-off after question four, and a sign-up CTA that offered nothing in return. We shortened the quiz and — working with Preezie and Mailchimp — built an automation that emailed your results back to you. A real reason to hand over your email. Conversion jumped to 15% in a single month.

PreezieMailchimpCRM automationFunnel analysisA/B testing
8–9% → 15%Sign-up conversion rate
70%+Improvement, one month

The problem was never the traffic. It was the value exchange. Diagnosing that correctly was worth more than doubling the ad spend would have been.
